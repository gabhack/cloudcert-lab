Frequently Asked Questions

How much do AWS, Azure AI-901, and Google Cloud GenAI Leader exams cost in 2026–2027?

Foundational certifications cost $99 USD (Microsoft Azure AI-901 and Google Cloud Generative AI Leader) or $100 USD (AWS CLF-C02 and AWS AIF-C01). AWS Associate exams (DVA-C03, DVA-C02, SAA-C03, DEA-C01, MLA-C01) cost $150 USD, and AWS Professional exams (AIP-C01) cost $300 USD per attempt.

Do AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud give free exam retakes?

No. You must pay the full exam fee again on every retake attempt unless you hold a promotional voucher. AWS enforces a 14-day waiting period after a failed attempt, Microsoft requires 24 hours after the first fail (14 days after subsequent fails), and Google Cloud requires 14 days after the first fail (60 days after the second).

How does passing a Foundational AWS exam cut Associate and Pro exam costs by 50%?

Passing any AWS certification (such as CLF-C02 or AIF-C01 for $100) grants a 50% discount voucher in AWS Certification Benefits, reducing a $150 Associate exam (DVA-C03, SAA-C03, DEA-C01, MLA-C01) to $75 or a $300 Professional exam (AIP-C01) to $150.
